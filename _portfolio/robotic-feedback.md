An experimental neural interface needs a coordinated path from a movement cue to brain recording, signal interpretation, and an appropriate response. My systems work connects these components using humanoid robots.

## EEG-monitored action demonstrations

Our ICORR 2025 paper describes a gripper-equipped humanoid social robot integrated with EEG recording for action-observation experiments. Three healthy volunteers watched and imitated movements presented on video or by the robot in person. The study demonstrates the experimental integration and explores differences between delivery conditions.

## From decoding to robot feedback

In ongoing prototype work with Zihua Sun, Dorian Efrati, and Michelle J. Johnson, we connect EEG replay, preprocessing, adapted model inference, decision gating, and high-level robot control. A proof-of-concept evaluation replayed 1,281 selected trials from 30 stroke participants to drive robot gestures.

This is a replay-based technical evaluation. Live participant-facing operation, calibration, usability, and rehabilitation outcomes remain subjects for future study.
