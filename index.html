Bioengineering · University of Pennsylvania

I study how brain activity can inform more personalized rehabilitation and human–robot interaction.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Bioengineering at the University of Pennsylvania, advised by Dr. Michelle J. Johnson in the Rehabilitation Robotics Lab. My research combines electroencephalography (EEG), machine learning, and robotic systems to investigate motor engagement and feedback for stroke rehabilitation.

My work spans EEG responses during action observation, adaptation of EEG foundation models for motor imagery decoding, and the design and evaluation of robot-mediated rehabilitation interfaces. I publish under the name Anh T. Nguyen.
