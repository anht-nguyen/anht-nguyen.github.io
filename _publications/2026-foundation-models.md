## Research summary

We evaluated low-rank adaptation of pretrained EEG models for left- versus right-hand motor imagery decoding using healthy-participant recordings and data from 30 stroke participants.

Direct healthy-to-stroke transfer performed near chance. Adaptation improved performance for some models, but subject-level variability remained important. The results support evaluating calibration and reliability for individual users before integrating decoded outputs into rehabilitation feedback.

This work is listed as a preprint, separately from peer-reviewed publications.
