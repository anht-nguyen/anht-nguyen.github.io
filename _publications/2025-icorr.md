## Research summary

This paper describes an experimental platform that synchronizes EEG recording with upper-limb action demonstrations by a gripper-equipped humanoid robot.

Three healthy volunteers watched and imitated actions shown through video or by the robot in person. The study establishes technical feasibility and explores individual neural responses across delivery conditions.
