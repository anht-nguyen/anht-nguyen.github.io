## Research summary

This pilot study analyzed sensorimotor EEG responses while three healthy adults watched actions performed by a human actor and a humanoid robot.

Responses varied across participants, supporting the feasibility of measuring individual differences and motivating further study of how movement demonstrations engage neural activity.
