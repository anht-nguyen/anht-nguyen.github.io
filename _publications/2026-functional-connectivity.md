## Research summary

This exploratory study compared five EEG functional-connectivity metrics for decoding observation conditions and action types in five healthy adults. The analysis examined conventional classifiers and neural models, including graph neural networks.

The findings motivate careful feature selection, including the treatment of zero-phase-lag effects and directed interactions. Larger studies are needed to evaluate how well these results generalize.

A related conference abstract appeared in *Circulation* in 2025. This manuscript is listed separately as a 2026 preprint.
