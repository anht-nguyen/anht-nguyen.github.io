## Research summary

This survey examined rehabilitation therapists’ reported communication practices before and during the pandemic, along with their plans for later use. Video communication increased substantially, but therapists identified constraints on which activities could be delivered remotely.

The work provides practical context for selecting tasks and interfaces in telerehabilitation research.
