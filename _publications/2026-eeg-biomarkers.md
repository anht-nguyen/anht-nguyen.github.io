## Research summary

This review examines EEG-derived markers during upper-limb action observation after stroke, including sensorimotor rhythms and functional connectivity, and their reported relationships with motor outcomes.

The synthesis identifies promising measures of neural engagement while highlighting differences in stimuli, recording procedures, and outcome definitions. Standardized protocols and further validation are important for evaluating these measures in individualized rehabilitation.
