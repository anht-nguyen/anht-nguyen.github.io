## Research summary

This earlier work investigates direct collocation as an approach to optimal control for multimodal assistance in a simulated lower-limb assistive system.

The project grew from my undergraduate research with Vincent Bonnet and Samer Mohammed and provides an engineering foundation for my interests in movement assistance and rehabilitation robotics.
