## Research summary

Participants experienced simulated rehabilitation interactions in three formats: face-to-face, conventional telepresence, and social robot-augmented telepresence.

Although face-to-face interaction was preferred overall, 71% of participants preferred robot-augmented interaction over conventional video. Preferences varied with age and motor and cognitive function, illustrating the importance of designing rehabilitation interfaces around individual needs.
