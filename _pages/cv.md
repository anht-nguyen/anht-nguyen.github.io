## Research experience

**Graduate Research Assistant** — University of Pennsylvania, 2021–present

Research in the Rehabilitation Robotics Lab on EEG during action observation, motor imagery decoding, and robot-mediated rehabilitation interfaces. My work combines signal analysis and machine learning with experimental systems and human–robot interaction studies.

**Undergraduate Research Intern** — Université Paris-Est Créteil, France, 2019

Research on optimal control for lower-limb assistive systems, leading to a simulation study published in *Wearable Robotics: Challenges and Trends*.
